I was never a good writer, my mind and my soul is too minimalistic for yapping and blah blah. I prefer always going straight to the point in the shortest possible way. No intro, no ending, just a core of the solution. I'm a problem solver.

With the current LLMs I can delegate that incapacity to a computer. They can write better than me and I don't even need to think about what I need to write. I just need to describe a broad idea.

THIS IS NOT WHAT I AM DOING HERE.

## Comfort zone of introvert

The COVID times were the best times of my life. Maybe not at the beginning but after a few months, when everything was closed, everyone in my industry went into the remote work, I get back my kids from my ex (because she wasn't able to provide for them nor have them at home 24/7) and the job offers were coming left and right.

I've been able to stay in the home office, focus on those beautiful puzzles which one we've been solving with the code, didn't have to talk much with anyone, locked deep inside my mind. When I had to relax and recharge my mental batteries, I was jumping on my longboard to cruise empty streets of the London. No one did bother me, solution for every problem was in my hands.

After a few months I did notice that my never good communication skills getting even worse to the point that I wasn't able to describe simple things. Ability to imagine and understand complicated systems and concepts have nothing to do with ability to describe them. This is a big problem when you work in the team. There are times when you need to describe your design to other colleagues, maybe you need to persuade your idea. When all you got is: "trust me, I know what I'm doing. I'll give you a solution." You are on the losing position. It helps to speak with intelligent people which one can feel your gaps in speaking with their understandings of the topic, but if you want to be independent, this is just cheating that will not help you in the long term.

## This is how I was, AND NOW I'M NOT!

On every job interview I was half joking that "I'm better at speaking with computers than with the humans". It was true.

First time I've seen GPT-3.5 chat I recognize this great opportunity to have my own personal tutor. Everyone were trying to use it for the coding and other bizarre things, what by the way it was rubbish at that time, and I was right about saying that we are not there yet. I was using it for what it was. It was a language model, a model that can help me to improve a language. I could finally move abstract thoughts from my mind to the virtual world and have instant response. It was good enough to fill my gaps and understand relatively easy and broad topics. I didn't need to worry that it will be not interested in what I'm saying nor that I will hurt its feelings. On top of that it was this new thing to learn how it behaves, what it is doing, how it does the things. The exploration.

After a two years I did notice that I have less difficulties to write with people. At that time I decided that I need to slow down with sharpening my hard skills and put more time into improving my soft skills. It was a hard decision, but I'm used to do hard things.

Another big breakthrough was improvement of the TTS models. I can finally train not only what I want to say, but also how to say it. If the TTS can't transcribe my words then it's a clear signal for me that I'm not pronouncing it correctly. Also when writing I can have those half finished sentences on which one I can pause think and finish. When something's not right I can delete a word or two and fork my thinking process. I don't think I can do it with the is when speaking. You see as a coder programmer developer or engineer I'm used to dirty drafts on which one I iterate a lot and very quickly. I'm just throwing ideas out of my head and then quite often I'm changing the code 180 degrees sometimes even deleting it and doing it again. When the word was spoken, it can't really be unspoken. This is a different concept for me. Just another skill I'm learning.

Of course I use agents in the pretty popular way at least along a good developers for brainstorming code assistance or even generating full solutions. There is quite a few hard skills we need to learn in this new universe, But this is topic for another day.

## Whats the point or doing it yourself?

Why to bother to write an article spend an hour or so, when instead I can just give one sentence to the LLM and got a full book (what BTW I've done few times >;-] ).

> Give me six hours to chop down the tree, I'll spend first four on sharpening the axe

- A. Lincoln

I like the numbers, I like the math, laws of physics... They are all provable and hard to question. It's not the case with the ambiguous language, where everything is just an opinion. Your true and my true might be completely different and none of us are wrong nor right (maybe we all are just wrong, who knows). This is very logical for me and to say at least not comfortable for some. But sitting inside the comfort zone is a slow killer. Even if it is just selfish desire to improve myself for the sake of fight with my imperfections, it's a good starting point. Having more skills is always better than having less of them doesn't really matter how many of them you need.
